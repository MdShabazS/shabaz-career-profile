## Overview

A college team project on STM32 Nucleo-L476RG that supports healthier desk habits through embedded sensing and feedback: presence detection, temperature monitoring, OLED feedback, break reminders, and buzzer alerts, driven by a deterministic event/state loop on STM32 HAL.

## Known technologies

STM32CubeIDE, STM32 HAL, ultrasonic sensing, ADC temperature sensing, I2C OLED, timers, Embedded C.
